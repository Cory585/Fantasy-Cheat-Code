Follow your ESPN draft live

Picks show up here a few seconds after they're made on ESPN, and the plan below updates around them. Your league ID is the number after leagueId= in your ESPN league's address.

Private league?

ESPN only shows private leagues to members, so this page needs two cookies from a browser where you're signed in to ESPN: open ESPN Fantasy, open developer tools (F12), and under Application (or Storage), Cookies, copy espn_s2 and SWID. They're used only to read your league's draft from ESPN, kept in the server's memory until you disconnect or for a few hours, and never saved.
